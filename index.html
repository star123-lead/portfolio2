PROTECT ME

AI & IoT · College Project

An innovative, advanced emergency alert system combining Internet of Things (IoT) sensors and Artificial Intelligence to detect danger states (such as critical falls, physical panic, or physical violence sound patterns) and instantly transmit real-time geolocation alerts to emergency contacts and local agencies. Designed as a college project with classmate Vaitheswaran.C.

Technical Features & Concept

  • ✦ Multi-Sensor Fusion: Links smart sensor endpoints (fall gyroscopes, heart monitors, panic buttons, and volume decibels) to gather physical emergency triggers in real-time.
  • ✦ AI Danger Verification: Integrates machine-learning heuristics to verify true threats, minimizing false triggers while assessing environmental risk indexes immediately.
  • ✦ Instant Geographic Dispatch: Transmits GPS coordinates and vital parameters instantly via fallback mobile gateways and cloud broadcast endpoints.
  • ✦ React Threat Center: Backed by a highly polished, responsive dashboard layout representing incoming user vitals, threat levels, and mapping tracks in real-time.
